# Listening Stats v3.1
A bug fix update for 3.0. Thanks to everyone who sent a report!
Please keep reporting bugs! Much love - Xander

### Fixed
- **"The specified index was not found"** on every stat. This hit everyone whose history started on v1.
- **Listening Stats showing as a thin strip** with some themes, like Lucid.
- **The play count pill not showing** on newer Spotify versions, or when the extension didn't load.
- **Plays not saving** when Spotify's storage is full (Marketplace can fill it).
- **Setup and tour not closing** for the same reason.
- **Rank by time** on free stats.fm accounts. stats.fm only sorts by time for Plus.
- Fewer false crash reports when Spotify can't be reached or its session expires.
