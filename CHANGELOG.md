# Listening Stats v3.0
3.0 is a full rewrite of the app (again). This time improving load times and app size by 60%!

### New
- **New dashboard** that fits your window, with Settings in a popup over the page.
- **Faster loading.** About 60% less code, and each part of the page shows up as soon as its own data is ready.
- **Several auto playlists**, each with its own source, period, size and schedule. They come back after a reinstall or on another computer.
- **Every period works with every source**, including the new "Last month".
- **Better share cards:** tracks, artists, albums, genres or stats, in story, square or wide.
- **Bigger World charts** with your own plays next to each entry.
- **Last.fm rows play like Spotify rows**, with covers, likes and the right-click menu.
- **Genre filter**, **Spotify history import**, and new settings like music only and skip private sessions.
- **Optional crash reports**, manual or auto.

### Fixed
- Songs from a private session or with tracking paused are no longer recorded.
- Switching stats.fm or Last.fm accounts no longer shows the old account's stats.
- Tracking no longer stays off when Spotify is slow to start.
- Large imports no longer freeze Spotify.
